Find all eigenvalues of the matrix

$$
A = \begin{pmatrix} 2 & 1 \\ 1 & 2 \end{pmatrix}.
$$

Recall an eigenvalue $\lambda$ satisfies $\det(A - \lambda I) = 0$. Compute the
characteristic polynomial, find its roots, and submit the **set** of
eigenvalues as your final answer (e.g. `{1, 3}`).
